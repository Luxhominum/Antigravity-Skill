## 1. CORE DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Every piece of poster or visual art design must adhere to these foundational rules:

### A. Intentional Hierarchy (The 3-Second Rule)
1. **Dominant Focal Point (Level 1)**: One hero element that commands immediate attention (a massive typographic statement, surreal art piece, or graphic silhouette).
2. **Context & Narrative (Level 2)**: Sub-headers, dates, artist names, or thematic slogans with strong typographic contrast.
3. **Micro-Details (Level 3)**: Coordinates, grid labels, small mono-spaced metadata, barcode accents, curator notes, and technical specs that reward close inspection.

### B. Composition & Grid Systems
* **Swiss / International Typographic Style**: Asymmetrical balance, strict mathematical modular grids, flush-left rag-right typography, ample negative space.
* **Brutalist / Industrial Print**: Raw mechanics, bold bounding boxes, crosshair registration marks, halftone dither screens, structural exposure.
* **Cinematic / Atmospheric**: Dramatic lighting, cinematic grain, wide aspect ratios within vertical frames, color grading inspired by 35mm film.
* **Editorial & High-Fashion**: Extreme scale contrast (ultra-large display serif paired with needle-thin sans-serif), generous breathing room, luxurious monochromatic palettes with single high-impact accents.

### C. Color Theory & Palette Restraint
* Never use random rainbow palettes. Maximize visual tension with **2–3 dominant colors** + neutrals.
* High-impact pairings:
  - *Dark Charcoal (#111111) + Acid Volt Yellow / Electric Green*
  - *Warm Cream / Newsprint Off-White + Deep Vermillion Orange + Ink Black*
  - *Cobalt International Klein Blue + Crisp Paper White*
  - *Monochrome Black & Silver Chrome with subtle grain*

## 2. OUTPUT WORKFLOWS

When the user asks for poster or art design assistance, provide:

### 1. Conceptual Art Direction
- Theme, mood, and core metaphor.
- Stylistic genre (e.g., Swiss Modernism, Cyber-Editorial, 90s Acid Rave, Deconstructivist, Bauhaus, Minimalist Cinema).
- Curated color palette with Hex codes.

### 2. Typographic Blueprint & Copy Hierarchy
- Display Typeface suggestions (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk, PP Neue Montreal, Ogg, Editorial New, Syne, Monument Extended, Space Mono).
- Exact text layout blueprint (Hero Title, Secondary Copy, Metadata blocks, Callouts).

### 3. Image Generation Prompts (for DALL-E / Midjourney)
Deliver mastercrafted image prompts with precise art-direction keywords:
- Aspect ratio (e.g., `--ar 2:3` or `--ar 3:4` or vertical poster formats).
- Medium & camera specs: *risograph print, 35mm grain, screenprinted ink texture, subtle scanline distortion, matte archival paper texture, tactile embossed finish*.
- Clear exclusion of generic AI tropes (no blurry gradients, no messy unreadable fake text).
